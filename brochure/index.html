Security & privacy

Built to keep a school’s data private

Each school’s data lives in its own database, and every request is checked for who is asking and which school they belong to.

A database per school

No shared tables of school data and no filter to forget. A sign-in from one school cannot open another.

Strong sign-in

Passwords are hashed with Argon2id. Access is short-lived, refresh tokens rotate, and repeated failures lock the account.

Role, scope and action

Every record is checked against what the person’s role may do. Records out of scope are answered as not found.

Verified family links

A parent sees a child only after the school has verified the link.

Audit trail

Who changed what, and when, with secrets redacted. Records are archived rather than deleted.

Private notifications

Push messages never carry fees, marks or leave details, and recipients are resolved on the server.

Safe on the phone

HTTPS only, sign-in tokens in the device’s secure storage, a biometric app lock and privacy screens on sensitive pages.

Files and money

Each file is checked before it is served, privately. Payments use idempotency keys and verified webhooks.

On the roadmapTwo-factor sign-in for staff · app-integrity checks on the phone · object storage for files · an independent penetration test before go-live

Pricing & licence

Per student, per academic year

One simple unit. A school pays for the students enrolled in an academic year. Staff, parents and administrator accounts are free and unlimited.

Only students are counted

Teachers, parents and office accounts never use a seat.

Counted once a year

A student is billable once per academic year, whatever happens to their section or parents. Promotion creates next year’s seat.

Seats that can grow

A school commits to a number of seats. At the limit its terms either block new admissions or bill the extra students at the end.

Never locked out

When a year ends there is a grace period of full access (30 days by default), then the school becomes read-only. Renewing restores editing at once.

GraceFull accessWith renewal warnings

The price per student and the number of seats are agreed with each school. Usage is counted automatically through the year.

Going live

From an empty site to a school in use

Four steps take a school from nothing to teachers, students and parents using it every day.

1

Create the school’s site

One command creates the school’s own database, its default roles and its first administrator. Add the name, colour and logo.

2

Bring the people in

Import students and staff from CSV. Parents are linked to their children and the school verifies each link.

3

Set up academics and fees

Years, grades, subjects, timetable and fee structures, guided by the setup checklist on the management dashboard.

4

Switch on the apps

Teachers, students and parents sign in on the web or in the mobile app, which asks for the school once.
